# Invoice document layout direction

Moneymatters invoice, estimate and receipt documents use a professional accounting-document hierarchy: business logo/tagline/name at the top left, document title and metadata at the top right, Bill To and From blocks beneath, a readable line-item table, totals, payment details, notes/terms, and footer.

The visual direction is inspired by the information hierarchy of established SMB invoicing products while retaining Moneymatters branding and controlled document preferences.
